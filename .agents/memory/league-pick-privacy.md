---
name: League pick privacy
description: Visibility rule for selections shown to other players.
---

Do not reveal a player's selected team or its TBD status on the League Picks
page until that selected team's game has started. Reveal it at kickoff rather
than waiting for the entire week to begin or finish.

**Why:** Players whose games start later must not gain an advantage by seeing
other players' still-changeable selections.

**How to apply:** Enforce this rule in the shared League Picks data builder so
both current-week cards and the full grid follow it. Completed outcomes and
automatic missed-pick losses remain visible.
